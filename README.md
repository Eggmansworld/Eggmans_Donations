## 💛 To Everyone Who Has Bought Me a Coffee

Thank you. Truly.

Every project I share is made in spare hours: late nights, weekends, and a lot of
"just one more fix." When someone stops by and leaves a coffee on
[Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/), it means more than the dollar amount
ever could. It says *this was useful to me*, *keep going*, and *I see the work you're doing*.

You didn't have to. Nobody asked you to. You chose to be generous to a stranger on the
internet, and that kind of quiet kindness is what keeps open, shared, community-driven
work alive. Whether you gave once or many times, whether you left a note or stayed
anonymous: **you are appreciated, you are remembered, and you made a difference.**

This repository exists so that your generosity keeps going after it reaches me.

## 🏛️ Why the Internet Archive?

The [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) is a non-profit digital library with a simple,
enormous mission: **universal access to all knowledge.** For decades it has quietly saved
what the rest of the world forgets: websites through the Wayback Machine, books, software,
games, music, film, and manuals for hardware that no longer exists anywhere else.

Preservation is close to my heart. So much of what I do relies on things that someone,
somewhere, took the time to save. The Internet Archive does that work at a scale no single
person ever could, and they do it for everyone, for free.

So once a year, I withdraw what you've kindly given through Buy Me a Coffee and pass it
on to them. It's my way of saying thank you to an organization that has given so much to
data preservation, and of making sure your support helps far more than just me.

> *Your coffee keeps my projects going. Your coffee also helps keep the past from disappearing.*

## 🔁 How It Works

```
   ☕  You buy me a coffee on BMAC
        │
        ▼
   💵  Donations add up over the year (Nov → Oct)
        │
        ▼
   🏦  I withdraw the balance once a year (BMAC + payout fees, converted USD → CAD)
        │
        ▼
   🏛️  I donate the equivalent amount to archive.org, rounded to the nearest dollar
        │
        ▼
   📖  I record it all right here
```

**A note on the numbers:** Buy Me a Coffee pays out in Canadian dollars, and the Archive
accepts donations in US dollars, so currency conversion and processing fees mean the
amounts won't match to the penny. I donate as close to the withdrawn amount as I can,
rounded to the nearest whole dollar. No screenshots or receipts here; this is simply an
honest record kept in good faith.
